The method

How it works

Four small steps keep the decision understandable.

  1. 01

    Redact

    Typed placeholders remove sensitive values before parsing.

  2. 02

    Sequence

    Group HDFS events by block or place BGL into time windows.

  3. 03

    Score

    Fuse rarity, count patterns, and optional sequence-model signals.

  4. 04

    Review

    Show the expected pattern, observed event, and score components.
